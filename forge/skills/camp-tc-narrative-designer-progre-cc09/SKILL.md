name: camp-tc-narrative-designer-progre-cc09
description: When reviewing another designer's progression/retention draft against demo telemetry or any evidence pack, before accepting unlock orders or retention targets.
---

1. Extract every empirical claim in the draft (unlock order, difficulty ramp, session shape, retention %, motivation sources). For each, locate the specific evidence line that supports or contradicts it; if none exists, write "no / not enough evidence" and name the measurement that would settle it. 2. Reject any retention % imported from a different platform/genre benchmark (e.g. mobile casual → WebXR). Benchmarks are hypotheses, never KPI promises; replace with an instrumented pilot and a falsifier. 3. Check whether the "trailer moment" and the "first-session teaching moment" are being conflated. If the flashiest move is also the move most new players fail, decouple: capture the trailer in scripted/spectate/replay mode and make the move a later mastery payoff. 4. Rebuild the difficulty curve from measured accuracy deltas (e.g. 120 BPM 71% vs 150 BPM 38% = cliff). Each stage must name the mechanic taught and an explicit unlock trigger (demonstrated competence), not just a level number. 5. Audit the draft for omissions: demand signals in the telemetry that the draft never addresses (e.g. social compare requests) are the most likely real retention drivers. 6. Give every hypothesis a measure (observation method) and a falsifying result. 7. Note gaps in the telemetry itself (e.g. events not paired per player, hypothetical survey questions) — do not infer beyond what is recorded.
