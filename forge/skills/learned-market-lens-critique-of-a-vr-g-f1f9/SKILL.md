description: On a critique panel judging a game deliverable's market case: hook, buyer, comparables, price thesis, store-page claims and the path to the revenue goal.
---

1. Read the market sections of the deliverable (GAME.md hook / Buyer / Comparables / Price / path-to-goal) and note every row labeled "not rechecked", "store search", "named" or "ARGUED" — those are unmeasured claims however honest the label.
2. Verify the comparables yourself with web search: each named title must exist, be a PAID title on the target store (free browser games and differently-genre'd titles are mechanic references only), and carry price + public review count from a named aggregator (Quest Store DB queststoredb.com, VRDB vrdb.app, Meta store). Record source + date. Reject a comps table whose rows cannot be upgraded in one search.
3. Ground the revenue goal with vr_sales_path_calculator: run it at the measured review count of the genre's BEST comparable and at the title's own realistic band. The gap between those numbers and the goal is the genre ceiling — the deliverable must state it in one line. "Only reachable for a top-decile title" with all inputs unmeasured is evasive, not honest.
4. Open the hero PNG and the title/first-impression PNG from pixels: does the image sell the one-sentence hook at 320x180? For a hand-driven game the hands/controllers must be in frame; material claims in the hook ("glass", "chrome", "neon") must be visible in the pixels, not just in the design doc. Check every PNG claim in the docs against the PNG (text-matches-image).
5. Re-open the evidence file any store claim rests on (beats, look delta) and quote the field — bot-measured optimal times are NOT player experience and must not become store copy.
6. File mustFix per defect with file:line + the concrete fix (replacement numbers, reworded claim, re-shot frame); put positioning and business-model moves in wouldElevate.
Mistakes to avoid: accepting "not rechecked" as a substitute for measurement; comparing only against free browser games; quoting the builder's self-check answers without opening the PNGs; letting the price thesis hide the genre ceiling.
