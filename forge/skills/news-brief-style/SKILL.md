# News style

- Neutral, factual, no editorializing. Attribute claims ("according to Reuters").
- Every item has at least one working source URL; prefer original reporting and official
  statements. Note when a story is single-sourced or unconfirmed.
- Include dates and locations. Use local-language sources when they are the primary source.
- Significance first: conflicts, elections and governance, economy and markets, disasters and
  public health, science and technology, climate, major business and legal decisions.

## Daily brief layout
`# World News Brief - YYYY-MM-DD`, then **Top stories** (5-8, two to three sentences each),
then sections by region (Americas, Europe, Middle East & Africa, Asia-Pacific) and by theme
(Markets & Economy, Science & Tech, Climate), each item ending with `[Source](url)` links,
then **Developing stories** (updates on items from previous days).
