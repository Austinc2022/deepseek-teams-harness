name: camp-tc-vr-head-design-contradict-e7d1
description: When asked to rewrite or repair a draft plan/spec that is known to contain internal contradictions.
---

1. Read the draft and list every claim as (claim, type: fact/decision/promise). 2. Cross-check claims of the same type against each other and against external constraints (market, platform, physics/team capacity). Contradictions cluster in: MVP scope vs timeline vs business model vs monetisation vs store policy claims. 3. Fix by making ONE explicit decision per contested axis and naming the rejected options with the reason (e.g. pick one monetisation model, not three). 4. Replace unmeasurable adjectives ("engaging", "looks good") with pre-registered numeric pass/fail criteria and behavioural proxies. 5. Mark externally sourced claims as CITED (with URL) or UNVERIFIED — never "verified, no sources needed". 6. Produce a "changes from draft" list naming each contradiction fixed; it doubles as proof of the audit. Mistakes to avoid: silently reformatting instead of deciding; keeping "nice to have" features in MVP; asserting store policy from memory.
