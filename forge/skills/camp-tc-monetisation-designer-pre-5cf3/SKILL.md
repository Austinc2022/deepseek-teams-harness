description: Asked to design or review monetisation for a premium (one-time-purchase) game and check revenue claims against market evidence.
---

1. Inventory every proposed purchase/mechanic; kill or replace anything that fails either test: (a) sells power/convenience in a paid game (P2W), (b) uses deception, coerced engagement, punishment streaks, artificial scarcity ("never again"), or resetting countdowns (dark patterns). Never guess on unstated prices/contents — list them as missing evidence.
2. Check revenue claims line by line with recomputed arithmetic (show units): gross vs net (store fee × refund rate as one net factor); halving mistakes in the claim; sustainable daily rate vs reachable install base (units/day × horizon vs addressable headsets); comparable titles' LIFETIME units converted to copies/day before any comparison. Label wishlist→sales conversion as an assumption range, not fact.
3. Produce a day 1–90 range from two anchors: (A) comparable daily pace prorated (outer envelope), (B) own wishlist × conversion band + preorders (base case). Report net/day and 90-day totals as a RANGE; cross-check totals sum correctly.
4. Premium-promise test: anything the store page already promises (e.g. "more content coming free") cannot be sold. Cosmetic-only, fixed-price, direct-purchase items pass; boosters, convenience skips, exclusivity claims, login penalties fail. Give a clean replacement for each rejection.
5. End with: verdict on the claim ('no'/'not enough evidence' allowed), specific data that would change the verdict (launch curves, demo conversion, featuring commitments, regional price tests), and name missing evidence rather than filling gaps.
