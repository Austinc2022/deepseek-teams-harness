description: When asked to audit or review a draft Meta Quest/Horizon store package (listings, prices, compliance, launch plan, owner checklist) before the owner submits.
---

1. Read the approved plan and QA build-facts first; they are the only truth for claims. Every listing claim must map to a build-fact or the plan; subjective puffery (dreamlike, relaxing, handcrafted) is acceptable, but measurable claims (session length, fps, features, comfort options) need a build-fact. Watch claims that come from the plan's *audience desires* (e.g. "10-minute escape") — a segment wish is not a verified product fact.
2. Coverage check per planned region/language: verify each locale has every store field (title, short, full, keywords, bullets) filled in that language. Empty placeholders, verbatim copies of another locale, and "(machine-translated, unreviewed)" text all count as missing. Also check for missing store assets (icon, screenshots, trailer/key art briefs).
3. Pricing: reject parity-by-number rationales. Check VAT/tax inclusion (EUR consumer prices include VAT; JPY includes consumption tax), FX assumptions stated explicitly, per-currency price tiers of the store, competitive reference set, and segment willingness to pay. Any price beyond the plan's fixed target is an owner decision — a draft must say "draft — owner decides", never "final".
4. MT/compliance: every MT string needs native review; check grammar of titles (German article/case agreement, Japanese transcreation not literal MT). Legal: never accept "GDPR does not apply" for a no-data app — platform/transparency duties and a store-required privacy policy remain; check local laws per region (Japan APPI). Ratings must come from the store's in-dashboard rating questionnaire (IARC; Germany shows USK, not PEGI) — self-declared ratings are not usable.
5. Launch plan boundary: flag anything that changes product scope (features excluded by the plan, e.g. leaderboards in a no-social plan), commits spend (paid campaigns, discounts), or is "final" without owner sign-off. Rewrite such items as options for owner decision or delete.
6. Owner checklist: compare against the store's real submission flow — signed release build, app record, in-dashboard questionnaires (age rating, data handling/privacy), privacy policy + support URLs, per-locale metadata, asset specs, pricing confirmation (MSRP + local prices), submit-for-review with lead time, review Q&A. "Upload and press publish" is always incomplete.
7. Verdict one sentence: ready/not ready + the top blocking reasons. Quote offending phrases exactly with a correction each; cite file names as evidence.
