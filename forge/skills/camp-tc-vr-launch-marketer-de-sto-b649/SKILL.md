description: Writing German (de-DE) store-listing copy for a calm/quiet game, or checking German consumer copy before owner review.
---

1. Keep brand titles untranslated unless the owner has an approved localised title; list the translated alternative as an owner decision only.
2. Calm register: short sentences, no exclamation marks, no marketing superlatives. "Ruhig", "sanft", "leise" carry the tone; avoid "einzigartig", "atemberaubend".
3. Common-term checks before finalising: fireflies → "Glühwürmchen" (colloquial, standard in games) not "Leuchtkäfer" (zoological); lantern → "Laterne"; "keep the light alive" → "Halte das Licht am Leben" (idiomatic).
4. Do not use statistics jargon in buyer copy: "median playtime" → "etwa N Minuten" or "rund N Minuten"; put the exact metric in the notes.
5. Input-device wording: "Tastatur und Maus" is safe for desktop browser; never translate "optional X" without the reliability caveat from the build facts (e.g. "experimentell, möglicherweise nicht auf allen Headsets").
6. "Subtitles" only when speech is captioned; for on-screen flavour text use "Umgebungstexte auf dem Bildschirm" plus "nur Englisch" when the build has no localised text.
7. End every draft with a notes block: changed/dropped claims, open ambiguities, native-reviewer items, legal/compliance items, and a DRAFT — owner review banner.
