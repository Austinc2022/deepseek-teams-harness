description: When rewriting or reviewing a product/design plan (PRD, scope, monetisation, store route) that another team drafted, before it goes to build.
---

1. Extract every factual and scope claim from the draft into a flat list (segment, MVP items, monetisation, fun, loc, store, demos, milestones, tech).
2. Cross-check pairs for mutual contradiction: MVP items vs segment ("busy people, 8 minutes" vs avatar editors/playlists); monetisation options vs each other (three models at once); stated timeline vs stated scope; "no risks" vs known platform limits.
3. Reject unverifiable absolutisms: "verified… no sources needed", "guaranteed by <library>", "RTL is easy", "no known risks". For each, either find a citable source or write UNVERIFIED. Store policy especially: search vendor docs (developers.meta.com etc.) and cite page-level, never rely on search snippets alone.
4. Replace fun adjectives ("engaging", "immersive", "players will love") with measurable proxies (time-to-first-rewarding-action, unprompted replay rate, completion rate, SSQ comfort delta) and state explicitly that only playtests confirm fun.
5. Bound open-ended words: "all major languages" → named territory-driven list with a per-locale cost number; "MVP" → ship list plus explicit EXCLUDED list.
6. Choose ONE monetisation model; name the price in 3 regions; write rejection reasons for the others (technical platform limits are the strongest rejection grounds).
7. Give every demo a hypothesis and numeric pass/fail set BEFORE building; include a comfort measure and a behavioural fun proxy (replay), never "pass if it looks good".
8. Replace vague milestones with named gates carrying exit criteria; add a risk register with owners; record a technical sign-off decision (full vs conditional).
9. End with a "changes from draft" list naming each contradiction fixed — it proves the rewrite was an audit, not a reformat.
