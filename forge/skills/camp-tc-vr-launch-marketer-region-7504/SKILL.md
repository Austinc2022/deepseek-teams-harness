name: camp-tc-vr-launch-marketer-region-7504
description: When asked to draft regional prices (local currency) for a VR/WebXR app store launch from evaluation notes and comparable-app price ranges.
---

1. Extract for each region: recommended anchor (if any), comparable-app price range, segment read (demand, price sensitivity, purchasing power, localisation expectations, refund behaviour). Never assume the studio's usual anchor; use only this app's notes.
2. Pick one specific price per region inside (not below) the local comparable cluster. Tie rationale to the segment: e.g. price sensitivity/refunds -> upper-middle of local expectation band but below straight FX conversion; niche with high localisation expectations -> mid-band (very low prices read as low quality and don't recoup localisation); lower purchasing power but cluster exists -> lower-middle of cluster, not the floor (avoid cheap anchoring); strong demand at top anchor -> entry of comps band to keep volume and discount headroom.
3. Sanity-check the discount path: compute 15% off each price; the discounted price should still land inside or at the edge of the local reference band. If not, prefer a lower uniform discount percentage rather than per-region percentages.
4. Mark EVERY price DRAFT and state the owner sets final prices in the Meta developer dashboard. Never submit, publish, spend or contact anyone.
5. List evidence gaps explicitly per region (missing payment data, unknown localisation cost, directional-only refund claims, qualitative demand, FX drift) and name one concrete thing that would strengthen each.
6. Add one paragraph of launch-discount mechanics (time-boxed ~15-20%, same percentage all regions, hard end date, "launch pricing" label) and one of what NOT to do (permanent underpricing, never-ending sale, mismatched tiers enabling arbitrage/devaluation, fake crossed-out anchors, promises of future features; listing claims must match the shipped build).
7. Keep the deliverable short and tabular; if the harness has no file-write tool, put the complete result in the final message.
Common mistakes: copying the US price wholesale into other regions; pricing below the local comp cluster; promising features not in the build; leaving the discount open-ended; omitting that prices are DRAFT.
