name: camp-tc-vr-launch-marketer-quest-8527
description: Reviewing any draft of Meta Quest (or similar store) questionnaire/compliance answers before they are handed to the owner for dashboard entry.
---

1. Read the verified build inventory and the current market plan (incl. any evaluation notes). Build facts beat marketing intent; market plan beats stale drafts.
2. For every draft answer, run three checks: (a) does it match the real build? (b) does it match the market plan (regions, languages)? (c) is it a legal/rating conclusion that needs a lawyer or authority (GDPR/LGPD/COPPA/age ratings)?
3. Classify each item KEEP (true as written), FIX (you can supply corrected text) or BLOCK (false declaration, legal call without review, or depends on unbooked input — corrected text stays a DRAFT). Any contradiction with the verified plan is at least FIX; a legal "does not apply" claim with no lawyer review is BLOCK.
4. Age ratings: never let a draft pre-select ratings (ESRB/PEGI/USK/ClassInd). The answer is "we complete the IARC questionnaire truthfully; ratings are assigned, not chosen", plus an expected range only as a non-committal estimate.
5. Privacy policy: assume a URL is required even for zero-data apps; the fix is a short no-data policy plus the URL placeholder, never "not needed".
6. Data answers: replace absolutes like "no personal data at all" with precise scope (nothing leaves the device; note any on-device user-entered content and flag unknowns, e.g. free-text high-score names).
7. Status answers claiming "ready to submit" get BLOCK unless every earlier item is KEEP and required artifacts (privacy policy URL, IARC certificate, legal sign-off) exist; replace with an explicit blocker list.
8. End with owner actions split into: owner must-do, needs lawyer, needs native review, must remain a draft. State explicitly that nothing was submitted or changed.
