description: When asked to audit a draft Meta Quest / Horizon Store listing package (regions, copy, prices, compliance, launch plan, owner checklist) before the owner submits.
---

1. Read the approved plan and QA build-facts first; they are the only truth sources for claims. Never let listing copy assert features, numbers, ratings, or absences not in the build facts or plan.
2. Coverage matrix: one row per plan region x language. For each listing file check every dashboard field (title, short, long, keywords, bullets) is present AND actually localized — flag empty placeholders and "copied verbatim from en-US" as missing, not covered. Flag machine-translated-but-unreviewed copy as blocked pending native review. Flag grammar errors in titles (e.g., German case/gender agreement) concretely.
3. Claims audit: quote the exact offending phrase, name which fact it contradicts or overreaches (e.g., "collect them all" implies tracked completion when no save/score exists; "kein Stress" is stronger than "no rush"), and give the corrected wording. Also list verified selling points the copy omits (e.g., no ads / no accounts / no IAP) as suggested additions. Marketing puffery ("handcrafted", "dreamlike") is fine; factual overreach is not.
4. Pricing: reject rationale based on market wealth. Require a method: US anchor from plan -> FX (state rate/date) -> local VAT/consumption-tax display convention (EU/JP prices are tax-inclusive consumer prices) -> local psychological price points and comps -> draft marked "pending owner sign-off", never "final". Check numeric sanity (e.g., a foreign price far below the USD anchor needs a deliberate rationale).
5. Compliance: never accept "GDPR does not apply because no data" — GDPR can attach to hosting logs/IP; correct to "designed not to collect data, wording needs legal review". Note the store requires a live privacy policy URL even for no-data apps. Age ratings must come from IARC/ESRB/PEGI/CERO questionnaires — self-asserted "draft, unrated" ratings cannot appear as final. Japan needs APPI/CERO/Specified Commercial Transactions coverage; "no local rules noted" is a gap. Name native-review and lawyer-review items separately.
6. Launch plan vs owner boundary: strike anything contradicting the plan (e.g., a leaderboard when the plan says no social features). Anything that spends money, publishes publicly, or sets/changing prices is an OWNER decision — reframe as a proposal with budget and owner sign-off line. Distinguish in-app "no ads" (plan constraint) from external marketing (not prohibited, but owner-gated).
7. Owner checklist: walk the real dashboard flow — org/financial account for paid apps, distribution-channel question for WebXR builds (store needs a packaged APK), device/play-mode/input metadata matching QA evidence, IARC + age-group self-certification + data use checkup, privacy policy/website/support URLs, all store assets (transparent logo, cover art rules, accurate screenshots, <=2 min trailer, no non-Quest hardware), per-language copy within field limits, regional pricing tools, VRC pre-checks (target refresh rate, pause, focus-aware, hand-tracker/controller switching, orientation reset), then owner sign-off and submit.
8. Verdict: one sentence, ready / not ready, naming the top blockers. Keep quotes exact; separate must-fix from nice-to-have.
