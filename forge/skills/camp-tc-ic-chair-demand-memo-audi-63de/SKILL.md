description: When auditing a prior IC/demand memo whose GO verdict rests on popularity signals, TAM fractions, insider surveys or related-party LOIs, and writing a corrected decision.md.
---

1. List every demand input: concept, single target segment, each evidence item with source and strength (measured > simulated > argued > hearsay). Missing input -> revise-once naming it.

2. Re-audit each prior claim down to primary numbers. Common reframes: (a) "waitlist of N" is usually collected emails - report collected vs engaged (opens) vs paid; never assert conversions the record does not state; the misstatement is usually the label ("waitlist"), not the count. (b) LOIs from friends/classmates/insiders are related-party: worth zero until an unaffiliated buyer signs a stated price with a non-refundable deposit (>=20%). (c) TAM fractions ("0.5% of $XB") are assumptions, not evidence - and check the memo's own arithmetic actually reaches the target (e.g. $300M/yr = $822k/day, which misses $1M/day). (d) Refund-adjust every sales claim: gross minus refunds = net; a refund rate over 50% is a negative demand signal, and refunds arriving after a review-solicitation email is product risk. (e) Survey claims where most respondents are on the team reduce to n = number of unaffiliated respondents.

3. Classify prototype completion as sunk cost / proof of execution, excluded from the demand decision by name. Popularity at zero price (views, opens, scores) is attention, not demand; only money that stays paid past the refund window counts.

4. Score on the venture-scoring rubric. Show the weighted column explicitly (overall = sum of raw x weight / 10) and print the arithmetic so no reviewer can read a mis-add; state the verdict word as exactly one of go / revise-once / stop. Realistic range misses both targets materially, or evidence is hearsay-only -> stop. Conditions that would force re-planning the whole venture -> revise-once, never go-with-heavy-conditions.

5. Build the revenue range starting from the observed NET dollars (e.g. "$10 net, ever"), then labelled estimates for y1PerDay / y3PerDay / ceilingPerDay with the formula shown. Compare each explicitly to both $1M/year ($2,740/day) and $1M/day ($365M/yr): state units needed at the observed price, convert through the observed refund/keeper rate, and give the multiple vs observed net revenue. Never average to a target.

6. Exactly one falsifiable paid test justifies a fresh gate: pre-registered, priced, unconnected buyers, with numeric PASS thresholds (net paid count, refund rate, day-7 activity) and a KILL threshold. Other verdict-changers must name findings with thresholds; list popularity metrics, insider surveys and TAM math as explicitly non-probative.

7. Finish with demo assumptions section: each unverified assumption the prototype/demo carries, written so it can be tested. Consistency check before handover: verdict word, score, conditions, falsifiers and demo assumptions must not contradict each other.
