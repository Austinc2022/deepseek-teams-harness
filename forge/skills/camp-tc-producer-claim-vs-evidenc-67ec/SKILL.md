description: When producing an integration go/no-go from a handoff claims document plus a separate evidence log or fixture.
---

1. Read both fixtures separately. Treat the handoff/deck as claims only; the evidence log is the sole evidence. A claim never proves itself.
2. Per specialist, classify each claim: Verified (log corroborates as stated), Conditional (partial or limited corroboration), Unsupported ("not enough evidence" + name who must supply it). Accurate but under-scoped deliverables are "verified as described" with the scope gap stated.
3. List at least three conflicts, each naming both disagreeing parts and the exact mismatch in numbers/records — not vibes.
4. Missing items table: each row needs an owner and a concrete artifact or test that closes it (a document, a capture, a re-pilot with a numeric pass threshold).
5. Conditional integration order: legality (licensing) first, then a build that can be measured, then measurements, then content, then the commercial case. Each step has a named gate with a pass condition; failed gates loop back to the owner, never proceed on promise.
6. End with a one-word full-build decision (yes/no) citing the specific numbers and records that decide it. Web research may add context (standard practice) but explicitly cannot replace missing measurements.
7. Traps: polished decks/art previews prove nothing about demand, licensing, or performance; stereo-speaker checks cannot validate spatial audio; desktop fps is a different measurement class than standalone headset Hz; a success metric without baseline + instrumentation is unfalsifiable.
