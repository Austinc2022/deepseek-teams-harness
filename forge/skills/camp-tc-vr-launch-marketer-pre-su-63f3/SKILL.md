# Pre-submission store package audit

1. Read the approved plan and the verified build facts first; they are the only ground truth. Claims in listings must match build facts; segment wishes in the plan (e.g. "a calm 10-minute escape") are NOT verified content facts.
2. Verify file contents before describing them: if you read several files in parallel, results can arrive out of call order. Confirm with one Grep (or serial reads) before ever claiming files are misfiled.
3. Region/language coverage: for every planned region x language, check title, short, full, keywords, bullets all exist AND are localized. Empty placeholders and English copy pasted into other locales are blockers. Note missing localized assets (screenshots, trailer, key art) and files present in the brief but absent on disk.
4. Claims vs build: cross-check every factual phrase. Flag unverifiable numbers (total playtime, fps on devices not QA-tested, "handcrafted"), grammar errors in titles, and comfort-feature claims (state only what exists, e.g. snap turn + vignette, never teleport if absent).
5. Pricing: US/anchor price comes from the plan; all non-anchor prices are DRAFT owner decisions. Reject "status: final". Demand a defensible method: price-tier mapping, VAT-inclusive note for EU (e.g. DE 19%), documented FX rounding for other currencies (e.g. JPY), and check the numbers actually follow from the stated rationale (parity claims must be arithmetically true).
6. Compliance: zero data collected does NOT mean GDPR does not apply; a privacy policy is required regardless. Hand-picked age ratings (ESRB/PEGI/CERO) are invalid — they must come from the IARC questionnaire. Check local rules per region (DE: counsel review/Impressum; JP: APPI, stealth-marketing disclosure). Unreviewed machine translation is a blocker; titles need native review too.
7. Launch plan: flag anything contradicting the plan or build (e.g. roadmap features the plan forbids: leaderboards, multiplayer) and anything outside the decision boundary (spend, discounts, contacting creators = owner decisions; note ad-disclosure duties per region).
8. Owner checklist: "upload and publish" is never enough. List concrete dashboard steps: agreements/payout/tax, IARC submission, data disclosures, privacy policy URL, localized listing + assets per region, per-region prices, device/distribution route, preview check, submit for review.
9. End with a one-sentence verdict: ready / not ready, and why. Deliver in the format the supervisor asked for (usually the final message for read-only audit tasks). Keep every correction concrete: quote the offending phrase, give the corrected wording.
