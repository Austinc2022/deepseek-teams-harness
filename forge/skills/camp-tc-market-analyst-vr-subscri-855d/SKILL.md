name: camp-tc-market-analyst-vr-subscri-855d
description: When asked whether a VR subscription app price × subscriber target can reach a revenue goal ($/day or $/year), or when a pitch justifies revenue with top-down TAM share.
---

1. Convert the revenue target to required paying subscribers: (target_per_day × 365) / (monthly price × 12). Also express as subscription payments/day (subs ÷ 30.42) and as % of the platform MAU base.
2. State assumptions explicitly: price, monthly churn (use 5–7% for consumer wellness subs; lifetime months = 1/churn), gross and net ARPU (net = gross × (1 − 30% platform fee)), LTV = ARPU_month ÷ churn.
3. Benchmark the required subscribers against hard internal anchors: best existing category performer, strong-new-title peak band, median app lifetime gross. Report the miss factor (required ÷ benchmark).
4. Check churn-replacement adds/day and implied UA spend at a plausible CAC; note whether the team/budget can sustain it.
5. Never forecast from TAM share ("1% of $1.5T"). If TAM is mentioned, convert to units and paying users first.
6. Verdict must be unambiguous: reachable / not reachable, by how much it misses, and what would have to be true (price ×N, platform ×N over how many years at the growth rate, or conversion % of the whole MAU) for it to flip.
7. Give a realistic annual revenue range for a well-executed entrant anchored to the benchmarks, with the downside named (median app lifetime gross).
8. Verify all quotients before writing (recompute required_subs, miss factors); never propose dark patterns or rule-breaking to close a gap.
Common mistakes: confusing gross with net targets; comparing a per-day target to lifetime gross without saying so; forgetting churn-replacement volume; treating peak subscribers as average active subscribers.
